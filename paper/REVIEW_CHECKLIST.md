# Human expert review checklist

Author: Durand Serge (Independent researcher).

This manuscript records existing negative results, formalizations and finite
evidence. AI-assisted drafting and document checks do not imply external review.
The human author remains responsible for the interpretation and submission.

- Check that every evidence key identifies the mathematical object actually
  represented by its source, and that the source hash and recorded commit scope
  are interpreted correctly. A copied source is not a new proof run.
- Check the analytic identities against their hypotheses: positive damping for
  the infinite trace, the alias guard for the finite projector, the principal
  branch and nonvanishing boundary term, and the true von Mangoldt series rather
  than an abstract replacement.
- Check the entire Gamma-inversion dependency chain and every row-level credit
  qualification. Partial batch credit must not be promoted to whole-batch credit.
- Check that the analytic official cumulative counter and the exhaustive
  declaration inventory are distinguished. Definitions and structures are not
  counted as mathematical theorems merely because they have axiom entries.
- Check the rational ordinary-polynomial setting, including both ordered
  off-diagonal terms and the midpoint loop, and the exact prime-coordinate
  substitution used in each algebraic statement.
- Check that the C0 calibration is circular and that the full-count calibration
  does not supply independent prime information. Direct coordinate pinning is
  distinguished from uniqueness of the entire Boolean vector at a finite input.
- Check the family table domains and uniform common-zero statements. A first
  failure in a tested quartet is not a global first input. The late saved-data
  gate result is finite EVIDENCE without new Trophy credit at the stop.
- Check that the Lean static Nullstellensatz result is a lower bound. Equality
  is supported only at the measured finite points; the universal loop upper
  construction is not established.
- Check all finite table derivations against their CSV/JSON inputs. The observed
  polynomial-calculus grouping by alpha is confined to the displayed sample and
  field. It is not an all-input formula or a characteristic transfer theorem.
- Check the explicit external KnapsackLowerBound premise. A literature citation
  is not a formal proof discharging that project predicate.
- Check the literal-cube, factor-horizon, divisor and k-ary common-zero scopes.
  Their restricted conclusions must not be read as exclusion of the full route.
- Check the integer/CRT finite computation, its quantization contract, retained
  prime powers, and its single-input scope. No native-program refinement in Lean
  or global signed analytic estimate is supplied by those observations.
- Check the stated gap between the published finite verification and the
  analytic onset. The paper does not fill that range.
- Check the frozen-target acceptance flaw: adding a proof to the bare declaration
  changes the frozen file bytes. The auxiliary build excludes that target.
- Check all bibliographic entries and MSC codes against the listed primary
  sources. The publication year of Selberg's conference contribution differs
  from the conference year.
- Check that historical Horizon material enters the established inventory only
  with the required existing Judge record, and that custom unproved axioms and
  physical analogies are excluded.
- Inspect the final PDF for clipped tables, unreadable theorem names, unresolved
  references, and missing bibliography entries. The document compilation check
  does not repeat a mathematical audit.
- Before submission, review the arXiv classifications, author contact, licensing
  of copied evidence, and the separate release plan for large evidence files.

Claims deliberately excluded or narrowed include a universal Lean equality for
the static minimum, an unconditional PC lower bound without its external
predicate, a universal PC formula from a finite grouping, compiled credit for
SOURCE-only Mellin links, newly credited global family thresholds, and any
claim resolving Goldbach or excluding an entire route.
