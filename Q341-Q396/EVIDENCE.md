## Publication scope

This directory publishes a documentary continuation, two 28-page synthesis PDFs, and selected historical reports. It does not install the Q-campaign Lean source closure, generators, data tables, `.olean` caches, or full research archives into the permanent build. No new Lean compilation was performed for this documentation update.

The reports retain their original checkpoint verdicts, dates, and local paths. Their relative source-file references refer to their original packages, not to files newly installed here. Text line endings are normalized to LF for Git; sealed predecessor ZIP files are not rewritten. Older `OPEN` verdicts are superseded only where a later report establishes the corresponding result.

In particular, Q391_CLOSURE_REPORT supersedes the earlier partial Q391_REPORT frontier. Q392 progresses through separate continuation and prefactor checkpoints, before the eight-atom Q393 closeout. Q395 final status is **six** historical rows, superseding intermediate counts of three or four without changing their sealed archives.
